# Vision, scope, and success criteria

**Status:** Working proposal · **Updated:** 2026-10-26

## The problem

Community groups near Pflugerville overlap in geography, families, volunteers, equipment, and event audiences, but publish and coordinate in different systems. Organizers repeat work; residents miss nearby events; free signup tools often contain advertising or impose costly upgrades.

## Intended participants

Independent HOAs, PTOs, schools (subject to district approval), MUDs, apartment communities, local nonprofit and civic groups. Initial conversations of interest: Brookfield Estates HOA and Wieland Elementary PTO, with potential involvement from Northtown MUD and additional groups. Inclusion here does **not** constitute agreement to participate.

## Product goals

1. **Events:** A public, no-login calendar of events that organizations explicitly authorize for publication. Ingest existing calendars when possible; provide a reviewable manual submission path otherwise.
2. **Coordination:** Help independent organizers communicate, cross-promote, share equipment/volunteer requests, and plan jointly without falsely implying another organization's approval.
3. **Resources:** Share practical, curated templates, vendor contacts (with permission), event checklists, and institutional lessons learned.
4. **Ad-free utilities:** Replace costly or advertisement-supported signup, registration, and similar services with OSS wherever feasible. Visitors should not need new accounts for ordinary signup or browsing.

## Initial scope

- Organization registry with source attribution and responsible organizer roles.
- Accurate public event records, original-source links, update/cancellation handling, and curator controls.
- Cross-organization collaboration pilot using existing software.
- Simple ad-free volunteer/supply signup links.
- Basic organizer document collection.
- Payments only as a later optional module using a connected account belonging to **each** organization.

## Explicitly out of scope for first release

- Forming or fundraising through a new nonprofit.
- Acting as agent for PfISD, a PTO, an HOA, or a MUD.
- Shared merchant account, pooled funds, or transferring funds among organizations.
- Student records, school volunteer screening, child registrations, or private staff directories.
- Replacing existing organizations' websites and internal systems.
- A fully custom social network, accounting package, or payment processor.

## Success criteria for a pilot

- A resident can discover a real approved event without a login and see its original source.
- A partner updates or cancels an event and our listing reflects the change, or is clearly marked pending review.
- Two independent organizers can coordinate one event without confusing who approved and owns which work.
- A nonmember can claim a volunteer slot without ads or unnecessary registration.
- Each organization's private signup and financial data is inaccessible to unrelated organizations.
- An outgoing HOA/PTO officer can transfer control without handing over a personal account.

## Open strategic questions

- Who legally operates the shared site, owns data, contracts for hosting, and purchases insurance?
- How broad is the geographic service area (Pflugerville proper, ETJ, PfISD, nearby districts)?
- How do organizations confirm participation and designate representatives?
- Is Hylo the coordinator hub, or should the pilot use simpler tools before a permanent hub?
